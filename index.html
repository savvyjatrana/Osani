🏬 About Osani.com

Osani.com is a global online mega store bringing together fashion, jewelry, footwear and home decor from artisans and trusted sellers around the world. Founded with a simple idea — that quality craftsmanship should be accessible to everyone, everywhere — we curate collections that blend traditional design with modern convenience.

We ship worldwide, including the USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Europe and India, and we back every order with secure payments, easy returns, and a dedicated customer support team.

Return, Exchange & Refund Policy

We want you to love what you order. If you're not fully satisfied, most items are eligible for return or exchange within 7 days of delivery.

Eligibility for Return

  • Item must be unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with all tags intact.
  • Certain categories (innerwear, jewelry sets that are opened, and customized items) are non-returnable for hygiene and safety reasons.
  • Damaged, defective, or wrong items are eligible for free replacement or full refund.

Refund Timeline

  • Refunds to original payment method: 5–7 business days after pickup/quality check.
  • Cash on Delivery refunds are processed via bank transfer or store credit.

To start a return, go to Profile → Orders and select "Return / Exchange", or contact support@osani.com with your order ID.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

  • Free shipping worldwide on orders above $50 / ₹3,999; a nominal fee applies below that.
  • Standard delivery: 3–7 business days within India, 7–14 business days for international orders.
  • You will receive tracking updates via SMS/WhatsApp and email once your order is dispatched.
  • Customs duties, if applicable on international orders, are the responsibility of the customer.

We value your privacy. Any personal information you share with us (name, address, phone, payment details) is used solely to process your orders and improve your shopping experience.
